Summary of the pipeline

  1. Detect columns on very different scales and skewed shapes.
  2. Apply Yeo-Johnson (λ per column) + standardisation, fitted on the training split.
  3. Obtain the rescaled dataset (same rows, comparable units).
  4. Validate skewness per column and order preservation (multivariate shape check).
  5. Decide accept records inside ±3 SD; flag extreme ones (10 accepted, 0 flagged).

Key takeaway

Yeo-Johnson puts every column on the same scale (SD ratio 21.7 → 1.0) and the shape check confirms it kept the order of patients; |skew| fell in 5 of 6 columns (Sleep h 0.30 → 0.96 rose: a discrete column with few levels cannot be made symmetric by a power transform).
