4. Out-of-fold label validation

How Label Validation Works?

  • A gradient-boosting model is trained 5 times on 4/5 of all 29,471 records and predicts the held-out 1/5 (never its own label).
  • Threshold t = 0.58 = mean P(case) among recorded cases (Confident Learning's rule).
  • A recorded non-case with P(case) ≥ t looks like a case → the label is suspicious. 3 of the 3 removed records pass this independent check.
  • Kept records have labels consistent with their features.

Summary of the pipeline

  1. Detect label noise in the recorded diagnosis.
  2. Apply Confident Learning (out-of-fold probabilities, group-wise thresholds).
  3. Obtain the cleaned training set.
  4. Validate labels with an independent out-of-fold model on all columns.
  5. Decide keep consistent labels, flag contradicted ones (7 kept, 3 flagged among the 10).

Key takeaway

Confident Learning finds recorded labels that the data strongly contradict, and the out-of-fold check confirms that the removed records really look like the other class, while recorded cases are never discarded.
